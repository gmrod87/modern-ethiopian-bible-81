# Hobah Ancient Library — Proofreading, Rights & Source-Integrity Pass

**Date:** 25 August 2026  
**Branch:** `proofread-rights-source-2026-08-25`  
**Scope:** the 17 Hobah Ancient Library translation/edition configuration files plus the existing Release 113 production-rights policy.

## Editorial rule adopted

Hobah must not make a copyrighted modern translation look "unique" through cosmetic paraphrase. Production English may be used only when it is:

1. newly prepared Hobah wording from a documented source-language witness that Hobah may lawfully use;
2. a clearly identified Hobah modernization/edition of a public-domain historical English witness; or
3. a clearly identified Hobah research reading/synopsis where the present prose is not a verified line-by-line translation.

Modern copyrighted translations and critical reconstructions may be consulted as scholarship where lawful, but their wording must not be copied into production merely to be rewritten.

## Labels used after this pass

- **Hobah Translation** — reserved for a genuine source-language translation tied to an identified recension/witness and suitable for line-by-line verification.
- **Hobah Edition** — a Hobah modern-English edition/modernization of an identified public-domain historical English witness.
- **Hobah Research Reading** — newly written Hobah explanatory/reader prose based on the ancient work, but not represented as a diplomatic or line-by-line translation.
- **Withheld pending source verification** — no production text until the rights trail and source witness are sufficiently documented.

## Findings and amendments

### 1. Life of Adam and Eve — critical recension correction

The former file was labelled as a Greek *Apocalypse of Moses* source-language translation while its opening includes the Jordan/Tigris penitence sequence and other material associated with different Adam-and-Eve recensions. The file has been corrected to **Life of Adam and Eve — Hobah Comparative Reading** and explicitly states that it is not a direct translation of a single Greek recension.

The public-domain Tischendorf Greek text may be used as a historical Greek witness, but it is not treated as a definitive reconstruction of the archetype. A future source reset should translate Greek, Latin and other major recensions separately rather than silently harmonizing them.

### 2. Apocalypse of Abraham — retained provisionally

The file already identifies itself as a **provisional source-witness research translation** based on the surviving Slavonic tradition and a public-domain early philological edition. It was not relabelled in this pass. Before Hobah ever calls it a certified direct translation, the English should be checked line by line against a stored/licensed Slavonic transcription and all corrupt/secondary readings documented.

### 3. Testament of Abraham — retained as recension-specific research translation

The file already identifies Greek Recension A and a public-domain M. R. James Greek edition and explicitly says the short recension is not silently blended. It remains a Hobah translation candidate, but a future direct-certification pass should verify every chapter against the Greek witness and preserve James's uncertainty markers where relevant.

### 4–9. Public-domain historical English witnesses — relabelled as Hobah Editions

The following files are no longer presented by title as though Hobah freshly translated the ancient language:

- Testaments of the Twelve Patriarchs
- Joseph and Aseneth
- Psalms of Solomon
- Biblical Antiquities (Pseudo-Philo)
- 2 Baruch
- Ascension of Isaiah

Their production method is a conservative Hobah modernization of identified public-domain historical English witnesses. This is legitimate provenance, but it is different from a new Greek, Syriac, Ethiopic or Latin translation and must be labelled accordingly.

For the Testaments of the Twelve Patriarchs and Psalms of Solomon, the pinned Scrollmapper files are treated only as a build/structure transport mirror. The rights claim rests on the underlying public-domain historical English witness, not on a blanket assertion that every contribution to the mirror repository is independently licensed. A later hardening pass should preferably replace these mirror dependencies with a directly archived public-domain source copy controlled by Hobah.

### 10–17. Dead Sea Scroll reader texts — reclassified

The following files contained newly written Hobah prose but included explanatory synthesis and, in some cases, section labels that could be mistaken for exact manuscript-column translation:

- Community Rule (1QS)
- War Scroll (1QM)
- Damascus Document
- Thanksgiving Hymns (1QHa)
- Temple Scroll (11Q19)
- Genesis Apocryphon (1Q20)
- Songs of the Sabbath Sacrifice
- Rule of the Congregation (1QSa)

They are now classified as **Hobah Research Readings**, not line-by-line translations. Where exact column labels implied stronger equivalence than the English justified, reader labels were substituted or the metadata was tightened. The files now state that explanatory transitions are Hobah editorial prose and are not words silently attributed to the manuscript.

A future direct Dead Sea Scrolls edition must identify a specific Hebrew/Aramaic transcription, manuscript/fragment siglum, line or column range, extant letters, restorations, lacunae and confidence. It must not copy a copyrighted modern reconstruction merely because the underlying ancient manuscript is public domain.

## Rights observations

Release 113 already contains a conservative production gate that:

- replaces standard/supported deuterocanonical wording with the public-domain World English Bible where applicable;
- retains R. H. Charles material only as public-domain historical English witness material;
- withholds canon entries where a sufficiently documented distribution-rights trail was not established;
- excludes the Platt/Sacred-Texts production branch conservatively; and
- scans production text for known modern-edition contamination markers.

That gate is useful but is **not an exhaustive copyright similarity search** and is **not legal advice**.

The Online Critical Pseudepigrapha distinguishes between public-domain manuscript words and scholarly reconstructed texts that may still be copyrighted. Hobah should therefore record the rights status of the *actual transcription or reconstruction used*, not merely state that an ancient manuscript itself is public domain.

## Proofreading/source-integrity checklist for every future Hobah text

Before a work can display the badge **Hobah Translation**, confirm all of the following:

- [ ] source language recorded;
- [ ] manuscript, recension or critical source recorded;
- [ ] production-rights status of that exact source recorded;
- [ ] English checked line by line against the source witness;
- [ ] lacunae and uncertain restorations visibly marked;
- [ ] variant recensions not silently harmonized;
- [ ] editorial explanation separated from translated ancient wording;
- [ ] no wording copied from a modern copyrighted English translation;
- [ ] completeness stated accurately;
- [ ] confidence stated at the level actually supported by the witness.

## Outcome of this pass

The corpus is safer and more accurate in how it represents itself. The pass does **not** claim that every surviving ancient manuscript has now been freshly retranslated line by line. Instead, it removes the most important category errors: a comparative recension is no longer called a single-recension Greek translation; public-domain English modernizations are called editions; and thematic Dead Sea Scroll prose is called research reading rather than direct manuscript translation.

That distinction is deliberate. Hobah should prefer an honest provisional label over a polished but overstated claim of originality or textual certainty.
