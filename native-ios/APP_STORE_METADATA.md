## Description

Hobah is an iPhone and iPad Bible reader built around the complete 81-book Ethiopian canon.

READ OFFLINE
The full Bible text is bundled with the app, so books and chapters open quickly even without an internet connection. Search the canon, change reading size and return to your exact reading position.

LISTEN
Use natural Read Aloud for Scripture. Background audio and lock-screen controls let you keep listening while your device is locked or while Hobah is in the background.

STUDY AI
Ask about the passage on screen for literary context, theology, cross-references, history and major interpretive views. Study AI distinguishes supplied text from broader historical or scholarly context and keeps spoken “explain that” answers compact.

VOICE STUDY
While listening, use hands-free commands such as pause, play, explain that and save that. Hobah can pause Scripture, explain the passage, read the explanation aloud and then return to the Scripture.

YOUR LIBRARY
Save verses, chapters, personal notes and Study AI explanations. Your reading library and position are stored on your device.

Hobah is designed as a focused reading and study tool: fast Bible access, natural listening, contextual study and a reliable private library without requiring an account.

Internet is required for Study AI and natural voice generation. Bible reading, local search and saved on-device Library content are designed to work offline.

## Review notes draft

Hobah does not require an account or login.

The 81-book Bible text is bundled inside the app and reading/search can be tested in Airplane Mode. Study AI and natural TTS require an internet connection.

Suggested review flow:
1. Open Genesis 1 or another book from Books.
2. Turn on Airplane Mode and confirm chapter navigation/search/local Library continue to function.
3. Re-enable the network, tap Listen to test natural narration.
4. Tap Study AI, ask a question about the current passage and use the Play button beneath the response.
5. Enable Voice Commands in the Read Aloud panel and say “pause”, “play”, “explain that” or “save that”.
6. Open Library to confirm saved Scripture/notes.

Microphone and speech-recognition permission are requested only for optional hands-free voice commands. The app remains usable without granting microphone permission.

Privacy Policy and Support are available from the native About tab and at the public URLs above.
