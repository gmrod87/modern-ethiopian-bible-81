HOBAH • THE ANCIENT CANON

Privacy Policy

Effective 25 August 2026

Hobah is designed so that ordinary Bible and Ancient Library reading, search, bookmarks, notes and reading position can stay on your device. Hobah does not sell personal information, does not use third-party advertising SDKs and does not use your reading activity for advertising or cross-app tracking.

Information stored on your device

Saved Scripture, Ancient Library selections, personal notes, saved Study AI explanations, reading position, text-size preferences and audio preferences are stored locally in the app. In the native iPhone/iPad version, these values are also mirrored into iOS app storage so they can be restored reliably. Hobah does not currently provide an account system or cloud-sync service for this library.

Study AI

When you choose to use Study AI, Hobah sends your question together with relevant Scripture or Ancient Library text and study context to Hobah's secure server endpoint. That endpoint sends the request to OpenAI's API to generate the answer. Hobah requests that generated Study AI responses are not stored as application state by the OpenAI Responses API. OpenAI states that API inputs and outputs are not used to train its models by default; depending on the API account, endpoint and applicable service controls, API content may still be retained for up to 30 days for abuse monitoring or legal/security purposes unless Zero Data Retention applies. Hobah does not intentionally write Study AI question or answer bodies into its own application logs.

Read Aloud

When natural Read Aloud is used, the text being spoken is sent to Hobah's server and then to OpenAI's text-to-speech service to create audio. The audio is streamed back to your device. Hobah does not intentionally keep a permanent server-side copy of the generated narration. OpenAI API processing and retention rules may apply to the text sent for speech generation, including temporary retention where applicable.

Voice Commands

Microphone access is requested only for optional hands-free features that need it, such as pause, play, explain and save commands. In the native iPhone/iPad app, speech recognition is performed through Apple's speech-recognition frameworks. Hobah's ordinary voice-command flow does not intentionally upload a recording of your microphone to Hobah's server. If a voice command invokes Study AI, the resulting text request is handled as described in the Study AI section above.

Network and service logs

Like most internet services, hosting and API infrastructure may temporarily process technical information such as IP address, request time, device/browser information, request path, status code and error details for security, reliability and troubleshooting. Hobah's current Study AI and Read Aloud server code does not intentionally log the body of your Study AI question, Scripture context or narration text.

Offline use

The native Hobah app includes the 81-book Bible data and bundled Ancient Library material inside the application so reading, local search and your local library can work without an internet connection. Study AI and natural voice generation require an internet connection.

Children

Hobah is a Bible reading and study tool and is not designed to collect personal information from children. Parents and guardians should supervise use of online AI features by younger users.

Your choices

You can use Hobah's core reading features without creating an account and without enabling microphone access. You can avoid transmitting study or narration text to online services by not using Study AI or natural Read Aloud. Local saved items can be removed from the app's Library.

Changes

This policy may be updated as Hobah gains features such as optional sync, accounts or additional native services. The effective date at the top of this page will be changed when the policy is materially updated.
