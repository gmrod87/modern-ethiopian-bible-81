Privacy Policy

Effective 20 August 2026

Hobah is designed so that ordinary Bible reading, search, bookmarks, notes and reading position can stay on your device. Hobah does not sell personal information and does not use your reading activity for advertising.

Information stored on your device

Saved Scripture, personal notes, saved Study AI explanations, reading position, text-size preferences and audio preferences are stored locally in the app. In the native iPhone/iPad version, these values are also mirrored into iOS app storage so they can be restored reliably. Hobah does not currently provide an account system or cloud-sync service for this library.

Study AI

When you choose to use Study AI, Hobah sends your question together with relevant Scripture and study context to Hobah's secure server endpoint. That endpoint sends the request to OpenAI's API to generate the answer. Hobah's Study AI endpoint is configured not to request storage of the generated text response. Do not place passwords, payment details or other sensitive personal information in Study AI questions or personal notes.

Read Aloud

When natural Read Aloud is used, the text being spoken is sent to Hobah's server and then to OpenAI's text-to-speech service to create audio. The audio is streamed back to your device. Hobah does not intentionally keep a permanent server-side copy of the generated narration.

Voice Commands

Microphone access is requested only for features that need it, such as hands-free voice commands. Depending on your device and operating system, speech recognition may be provided by Apple or the browser/WebView. Hobah's normal voice-command flow does not intentionally upload a recording of your microphone to Hobah's server.

Network and service logs

Like most internet services, hosting and API infrastructure may temporarily process technical information such as IP address, request time, device/browser information and error details for security, reliability and troubleshooting.

Offline use

The native Hobah app is designed to include the 81-book Bible data inside the application so reading, search and your local library can work without an internet connection. Study AI and natural voice generation require an internet connection.

Children

Hobah is a Bible reading and study tool and is not designed to collect personal information from children. Parents and guardians should supervise use of online AI features by younger users.

Changes

This policy may be updated as Hobah gains features such as optional sync, accounts or additional native services. The effective date at the top of this page will be changed when the policy is materially updated.

Support and privacy questions

Use the Hobah support page to report a privacy issue, technical problem or data-handling question.
