One home for deeper reading

Scripture, ancient literature, audio and study tools — without the clutter.

Hobah is designed for people who want broad access to biblical and ancient literature in a calm, modern reading experience. Move from Scripture to historical writings, save your place, listen hands-free, or ask for context without leaving the app.

01

The Ethiopian Canon

All 81 books. Fast, searchable and made to read.

Move quickly between books and chapters, search across Scripture, save passages, highlight verses and return exactly where you left off.

Ancient Library

Go beyond the familiar shelf.

Explore curated public-domain and rights-safe ancient writings, with fragmentary works clearly identified as fragments rather than presented as complete.

03

Read Aloud

A natural way to listen.

Turn reading into listening with natural narration, continuous playback and hands-free voice controls.

Study AI

Made for everyday use

Carry the library with you.

Core reading texts are bundled for offline access. Your saved reading data stays local on your device, while online features are used only when you choose tools such as Study AI and natural voice generation.
