Support

Help with the 81-book Ethiopian canon, Ancient Library, Study AI, Read Aloud and the iPhone/iPad app.

Reading and offline use

Reading, search or saved notes

The native app is designed to keep the Bible text, bundled Ancient Library material and your local library available offline. If a text does not open, fully close Hobah once and reopen it. Your saved library is stored separately from the bundled reading files.

Ancient Library

Fragmentary works such as the Book of Giants

Some ancient works survive only in damaged manuscripts. Hobah preserves fragment and section boundaries rather than presenting missing material as a complete ancient text. Read Aloud should continue through the sections of the current Ancient Library work and stop at its end.

Study AI

Study AI says it is unavailable

Study AI requires an internet connection. Check that your device is online, then retry the question. Ordinary reading should continue to work offline.

Read Aloud and Voice Commands

No voice or microphone response

Confirm microphone and speech-recognition permissions are enabled for Hobah in iOS Settings if you want hands-free commands. Natural Read Aloud requires an internet connection to generate audio. Background audio and lock-screen controls are available in the native app when audio is active.

Report a bug

For a reproducible issue, include the book or Ancient Library work, chapter/section, device model, iOS version, what you tapped or said, and what happened. Screenshots are useful.
